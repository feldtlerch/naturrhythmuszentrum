# Matrix allocations, loans and masked applications · `spec:nrz:matrix-loans-design`

**Principle (One numerical owner with movable allocations)** · `prin:nrz:matrix-loans-scope`

This design supplies the allocation, initialization, publication and retention mechanism required by (`req:nrz:tiles-streams-matrix-owner`). Matrix owns numerical storage and authenticates its occupations; bindings owns original meanings, required readings and companion relations. A loan moves exclusive ownership of separately allocated storage, while a published reader retains immutable storage. Neither route borrows the whole heap throughout execution. The existing exclusive execution route remains governed by (`rule:nrz:matrix-core-layouts`); its borrowed views do not become detachable. The mechanism uses safe standard-library ownership and the pinned backend's safe accessors, introduces no dependencies and changes no limit, default or existing tariff.

## Allocation owners and identities · `sec:nrz:matrix-loans-ownership`

**Decision (Separate owners for every lendable allocation)** · `dec:nrz:matrix-loans-allocation-owners`

A lendable value occupies its own precision-specific, contiguous `faer::Mat<P>` owner. Each companion has another such owner with its own component extent; each packed mask owns its unsigned-word vector. Layout maps and initialization maps are independently reserved native allocations. A private `OwnedTile<P>` groups these owners and their immutable declaration inventory; moving the group moves the owners, not pointers borrowed from an arena. Lendable tiles do not share a mutable slab with another occupation. Existing subregions in a shared legacy slab are not lendable: an explicit checked copy into separate owners is required. This chooses a bounded allocation/header cost over splitting a shared allocation or borrowing it through interior mutability, and preserves the storage format and checked layout laws in (`rule:nrz:matrix-core-resolution`), (`rule:nrz:matrix-core-layouts`).

**Definition (Occupation, bundle and loan identities)** · `def:nrz:matrix-loans-identities`

An allocation key contains heap scope, storage category, format, slot and nonwrapping occupation generation. A bundle identity additionally fixes its value identities/versions, every required companion identity/version and target selection, ordered original cell associations, component layouts, declaration/source revisions and all mask attachments. A loan identity adds a nonwrapping request identity and nonce. Private construction seals these fields. `LoanTicket` is a copyable diagnostic locator and has no ownership authority; `OwnedLoan<P>` is neither `Clone` nor reconstructible from a ticket. A reservation identity names the exact occupation it funds. Equal bytes, extents, nonce-shaped integers or mask populations cannot substitute for any identity. Foreign scope is checked before indexing, and exhausted identities refuse rather than wrap under (`rule:nrz:matrix-core-resolution`).

**Rule (Slot states and unavailable occupations)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-slot-states`

The managed allocation table distinguishes `Vacant`, `Reserved`, `Out`, `Published`, `Retiring` and `Exhausted`. Admission chooses the checked successor generation of a vacant slot, reserves all records, and installs `Reserved` privately; failed admission leaves no observable occupation. Issuing a loan moves its storage and reservation tokens into `OwnedLoan` and leaves an `Out` record containing its sealed identity, pending producer, reservation debit and liveness witness. `Out` is occupied for limits and unavailable for numerical resolution, reuse, replacement or compaction. Handback validation keeps that state while the sole owner is temporarily held by the handback frame. A successful commit changes the entire group to `Published`; cancellation makes it `Retiring` with explicit absence. Retirement frees payload and leaves `Vacant` with the last generation, or `Exhausted` when no successor exists. Neither cancellation nor failed handback makes a later occupant satisfy an old ticket.

**Rule (Reservation moves without releasing its debit)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-reservation`

Before allocation, admission reserves each value, companion, mask, initialization bitmap, map, owner header, table-capacity increase, publication staging record and cancellation record against the existing applicable ceilings. It counts initialized and unused capacity equally, including backend storage allowances and actual observable capacities without claiming to measure hidden backend overhead. Each allocation has one non-cloneable reservation token. The heap owns tokens in preparation, the loan owns them while out or being repaired, and immutable published owners own them after commit. The heap's bounded reservation index retains a debit keyed by each token throughout these moves; this is an accounting record, not another owner or another charge for the payload. Inventory includes all such debits even when their storage is outside the heap. Shared published allocations count once and their retaining edges count separately. In particular, the numerical allocator reserves a checked one-column capacity bound for the pinned backend before calling safe faer constructors; a later observation cannot authorize an already excessive peak. Recoverable allocation refusals use the existing fallible preparation paths; process-aborting allocator failure remains outside the atomicity guarantee in (`alg:nrz:matrix-core-publish`). Allocation and staging use the simultaneous old-plus-new peak; the scoped checks restore temporary byte limits on success and refusal and never refund Work, following (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-residence`), (`rule:nrz:plan-resource-ownership`).

**Rule (Pinned one-column slab capacity)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-slab-capacity`

Let L be the declared addressable scalar length of one slab, including any checked layout gaps; a contiguous C-cell channel with k components has L=C*k. The pinned faer primitive allocator rounds row capacity to its internal 64-byte alignment. For scalar width w in bytes, set a=64/w and R=a*ceil(L/a), then reserve w*R payload bytes plus its owner metadata before `Mat::from_fn(L, 1, ...)`. Compute the ceiling using division and remainder with checked native arithmetic, and preserve the existing signed-address preflight on the rounded byte extent. Binary32 therefore rounds to a multiple of sixteen scalar entries, and binary64 to a multiple of eight; L=0 has zero payload. The column capacity of this constructor is exactly one. After allocation check the safe public `col_stride()` against R; it exposes row capacity for this constructor, while private capacity fields need no access. A mismatch refuses admission. Only the L addressable entries are zero-initialized and charged as scalar initialization; backend padding is reserved but never read or treated as initialized tile cells. This is accounting for the pinned allocation geometry, not a public alignment promise or the separate solver workspace allowance. The constructor's allocator-abort/panic boundary remains that of (`alg:nrz:matrix-core-publish`); checked extent overflow refuses before entering it.

**Decision (Immutable shared owners and serialized heap changes)** · `dec:nrz:matrix-loans-immutable-sharing`

Use private `Rc` owners for immutable published allocations, layout records and retention records, with one registry root per owner and strong edges only along the acyclic use graph. The numerical payload has no `Cell`, `RefCell`, lock-protected mutation, mutable shared slice or interior mutable alias. Only the unique unpublished `OwnedTile` lends mutable access through its setters. A `BundlePin` retains the complete immutable bundle and its dependency closure; short views borrow that pin. Pin creation and duplication are explicit fallible heap operations that reserve their edge records, rather than unrestricted public `Clone`. Within a bundle, reciprocal value/companion target links and mask attachments contain IDs, not strong backedges: one bundle owner retains the complete mutually dependent group. Only declared uses of older bundles create outgoing parent pins. Weak references and locator tickets grant no read access and cannot be upgraded through the public interface. This is a single-owner API, consistent with the existing `Rc` contracts; it makes no thread-parallel execution promise. Registry mutation and publication require a short `&mut Heap`, while detached ownership and immutable views require none.

**Rule (Coexistence with the exclusive execution lease)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-lease-coexistence`

The heap routes tile occupations through this managed owner table and keeps their capacity in the same admission inventory as legacy storage. Tile tickets cannot be resolved as legacy dense `RegionView` or `NormalizedView` values, because those interfaces would bypass coverage. With a loan out, `Binding::validate` can still acquire its ordinary exclusive heap lease and execute on unrelated legacy allocations; owned masked applications can also evaluate already captured pins and their private output owners. An attempt to use the out occupation reports `OwnershipPending`. No loan, pin or detached application retains a `Bound`, `&mut Heap`, pointer into its slot vector or uncommitted `SlotPlan` across another heap operation. Handback and new admission wait until an existing Rust heap borrow ends. Global legacy relocation cannot relocate the managed owners or invalidate their per-occupation layouts; relocation or mutation of a managed owner is refused while it is lent or pinned. Legacy retirement, mask retirement and layout replacement must consult managed retention edges before touching shared owners. The implementation must not obtain this coexistence by weakening (`rule:nrz:matrix-core-layouts`).

## Safe filling and qualified coverage · `sec:nrz:matrix-loans-initialization`

**Definition (Sealed tile declaration)** · `def:nrz:matrix-loans-declaration`

Admission accepts a bindings-checked declaration of value and companion channels, their numerical component shapes and precision, original cell axes, checked cell/component maps, required companion incidences, both target maps of cross-companions, and supported payload validators. It fixes the requested input producer and absence scope. Native admission receives the capacity and layout selected by the engine; a host size hint is not an override, and tile-size selection remains subject to (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-tile-choice`). All stored components, including scratch and numerical companions, have the required binding coverage. Unsupported incidence or representation returns `UnsupportedContract` before lending. The host cannot add channels, change maps, replace targets or invent assurance after issuance. Different component shapes do not imply different original cell identities, and equal cell counts do not prove correspondence under (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-mask-coverage`).

**Rule (Setters track actual component writes)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-setters`

Allocate addressable numerical storage with safe zero initialization and packed tracking words with zero fill. Zero initialization ensures memory safety but establishes no input observation. Each channel has a private bit per logical component and a derived complete-cell bitmap. `write_component(channel, cell, component, value, account)` checks the declared location, charges its native step, stores through a safe accessor and then marks that component written. `write_cell(channel, cell, values, account)` checks length, addresses and the full write charge before changing any component, then stores all components and marks them written. Repeated writes replace the pending component without creating another cell. A setter reports `Written` or `WriteRefused(reason)` and borrows the loan mutably, so refusal cannot consume it. Bounds/shape/resource refusal leaves the addressed write unchanged. Floating payloads can be stored even when they fail later numerical validation; a NaN or negative radius is safe storage, not a publishable value. No host method exposes mutable mask words, marks unwritten cells complete, or yields a mutable slice whose subsequent writes escape tracking.

**Rule (Clearing and partial component writes)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-clearing`

`clear_cell(channel, cell, account)` clears that channel's component-initialization bits for the cell and invalidates its derived completion, without reading or needing to erase its old numerical bytes. It reports `Cleared` or `WriteRefused(reason)` before a failed change. This supplies an explicit way to omit a previously filled cell. Complete-cell bits are recomputed from all declared components; a partly written value or companion cell never becomes readable. No public API can declare a zero-filled spare cell initialized merely because its bytes are safe. Layouts used for writes must be injective within and across output channels; any reused read component still needs the checked reading relation in (`rule:nrz:matrix-core-layouts`).

**Procedure (Derive complete bundle coverage)** · `alg:nrz:matrix-loans-filled-closure`

For each value channel v, let I_v contain exactly its completely written cells; for each companion channel q, let I_q contain exactly its completely written cells. Start prospective value sets S_v at I_v. In stable declared channel and ascending cell order, remove a value cell if any required companion incidence is outside I_q, any target of that companion is absent from the prospective set of another value in this bundle, or any external target is absent from its pinned published coverage. Repeat full passes until no cell is removed. This descending computation terminates after at most the total number of initially complete value cells plus a final unchanged pass; charge every visit and incidence, including repeated passes. It yields the greatest jointly complete subset and handles cross-companions without assuming equal component shapes or replacing missing targets by zero. Set each companion's prospective mask to its initialized cells actually required by surviving value cells, with every declared target present; explicitly independent parameter outputs must be declared value channels. Orphan and incomplete companion cells stay private. A supported simpler cell-local incidence uses the same result with a single direct intersection. Checked maps, finite bounds and the canonical iteration order are part of admission, not inferred from bit positions.

**Rule (Partial and empty handback)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-partial`

Handback derives coverage by (`alg:nrz:matrix-loans-filled-closure`) without accepting a caller-supplied filled bitmap. It may publish any resulting subset, including an empty set with nonzero reserved capacity. Holes and incompletely written cells create no observations, fabricated zeros or suffix obligation. Validation reads payload only in the prospective value/companion masks; malformed numerical bytes in holes cannot become evidence. A completely initialized selected cell whose payload is invalid refuses the whole handback for repair rather than silently shrinking the mask. A caller can repair its components or clear it and retry. A valid partial handback closes this producer's unproduced contribution with its explicit absence scope; a consumer requiring those cells still has missing demand under (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-partial-tile`), (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-host-inputs`). Later filling requires a fresh input application and publication, never mutation of this immutable bundle.

**Definition (Qualified mask version)** · `def:nrz:matrix-loans-qualified-mask`

A plain native `MaskVersion` identifies packed bytes and their `CellLayoutVersion`; it is selection metadata, as described by (`[MATRIX-sec:matrix:packed-cell-masks]`). `QualifiedMaskVersion` is a sealed attachment containing that version, its role, exact bundle/value/companion versions and target-cell maps. A published filled role can be constructed only by complete handback or checked kernel publication. A demand or branch role may select fewer already filled cells but cannot upgrade coverage. Roles may share an immutable word owner while retaining distinct attachments. Changed bits need a new word version; changed targets need a new attachment even if the words are shared. Every channel has its own matching filled attachment, with padding zero and unmapped spare cells clear. Binding and reader access authenticates the attachments together, fulfilling (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-mask-versions`); `Heap::create_mask` alone supplies no initialization authority.

**Rule (Numerical validation and endpoint readings)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-payload-validation`

Validate selected finite centers, supported finite/nonnegative bound and error channels, target versions, coverage roles and all admitted companion invariants, including covariance shape, both target axes and source relations. A signed covariance entry is not subjected to a radius's nonnegative test. The validator dispatch is fixed by the checked declaration, never a host callback inside commit. Validate the selected numerical representation's exact decoding: stored lower/upper components are ordered finite endpoints; a center/total-radius reading denotes exact dyadic c−r and c+r and need not have representable endpoints in the storage format. A conversion requires its checked outward relation and discrepancy coverage. Where present, the numerical-only error reading remains separate from total radius and is covered by it; endpoint slack cannot replace it. These obligations preserve (`req:nrz:tiles-streams-matrix-owner`), (`thm:nrz:descriptor-binding-kernel-enclosure`). Handback establishes storage and declared input-assumption consistency, not containment in a sensor range, physical truth, or a new mathematical theorem under (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-host-loan`).

## Handback, repair and cancellation · `sec:nrz:matrix-loans-handback-state`

**Definition (Request and refusal outcomes)** · `def:nrz:matrix-loans-request-outcomes`

`request_loan` reports `LoanGranted(OwnedLoan, actual_layout, capacity)`, `RequestWaiting(DependencyPending)`, `RequestWaiting(OwnershipPending)`, `RequestWaiting(CapacityPending)` or `RequestRefused(reason)`. Waiting and refusal carry the request identity and retain no unreturned writable owner. A granted loan retains its pending producer and complete reservation. Closed refusal reasons are `ForeignAccount`, `ForeignOwner`, `StaleOccupation`, `DuplicateReturn`, `WrongLoan`, `LayoutMismatch`, `CoverageMismatch`, `TargetMismatch`, `InvalidPayload`, `KernelRefused`, `StaleDependency`, `UnsupportedContract`, `CountOverflow`, `ByteOverflow`, `IdentityExhausted`, `WorkExhausted`, `CapacityRefused` and `AllocationRefused`, with the existing underlying typed fault as detail where applicable. Capacity reports identify dimension, requested/actual size and retained/attempted usage. Payload reports name channel and selected cell/component without granting a read of another occupation. The other outcomes below reuse this reason vocabulary; they do not collapse ownership waits or numerical refusals into successful values.

**Rule (Authenticate before inspecting the returned payload)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-authentication`

`handback(&mut Heap, OwnedLoan, &mut ExecutionAccount)` first checks heap scope, exact slot generations and `Out` state, then request/nonce, the private ownership witness and reservation identities. It does not scan the returned numerical buffers or expose a current slot's contents before authentication. An attempt with a foreign owner, stale generation, already completed request or wrong nonce has no effects on the rightful loan, pending binding, versions or debits. `ReturnRejected(reason, same_loan)` returns ownership of the supplied loan in every such consuming case, allowing a genuine foreign loan to be returned to its rightful heap. Copyable ticket probes report `ReturnRejectedTicket(reason)` and can never publish or cancel. `DuplicateReturn` identifies a completed request still recorded in the bounded history; once that record is retired or its slot reused, `StaleOccupation` is sufficient and requires no unbounded replay log. Safe callers cannot duplicate the capability; private invariant controls must still test corrupt and repeated attempts.

**Procedure (Recoverable validation followed by atomic commit)** · `alg:nrz:matrix-loans-handback`

After authentication, hold the same loan as a private recoverable owner. Derive masks, validate selected payload and all companion targets, recheck pinned dependency/declaration revisions, and prepare the immutable owner headers, qualified masks, descriptor/source edges, operation evidence, retention edges and producer availability change. Reserve and finish every fallible allocation, checked arithmetic, Work charge and publication prerequisite before commit. Temporary masks and staging are additional reserved storage; the original payload, initialization map, pending producer and original debit remain intact. On any such failure return `HandbackRepair(reason, same_loan)` with the same identity, payload, initialization, exclusive access and cancellation capability, releasing only temporary staging. Spent Work remains consumed. There is no failed-publication branch that converts the error to a bare reason and drops the loan. This differs deliberately from discarding a private legacy candidate under (`alg:nrz:matrix-core-publish`), because the live host producer must remain cancellable under (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-host-loan`).

**Rule (No-fail bundle publication)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-publication`

Successful preparation yields a private prepared commit while the exclusive heap borrow remains held. Immutable publication headers are allocated as private empty owner shells before the transfer; their unique mutable access is established during preparation, with no external strong or weak references yet. Commit fills those shells through ordinary unique Rust access and only then exposes immutable shared owners. No allocation is deferred to wrapping a payload in `Rc` after the loan has been emptied. In that transition, move the numerical owners and reservation tokens out of the loan into those prepared shells, install every qualified mask and binding/companion edge, make the whole bundle available, and remove the loan edge. No allocation, budget check, caller callback, backend call or fallible pin creation remains after the move. After the filled attachments are installed, destroy the now-unneeded component-initialization maps and temporary staging with their prepaid cleanup, and release their debits in the prepared accounting change. Return `HandbackPublished(PublishedTile, PublicationReceipt)`, including the initial reserved reader/binding pin and actual filled attachments. Consumer dependency release is an effect of that complete publication and cannot precede it. The old writable loan is consumed once; retained tickets cannot recreate it. Publication is serialized transactional visibility, not lock-free concurrent publication, as in (`alg:nrz:matrix-core-publish`).

**Rule (Cancellation does not validate numerical contents)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-cancellation`

`cancel_loan(&mut Heap, OwnedLoan, &mut ExecutionAccount)` uses the same ownership authentication and returns either `LoanCancelled(CancellationReceipt)` or `CancelRejected(reason, same_loan)`. A genuine cancellation checks no payload, initialization completeness, sensor range, companion numerics or remaining evaluation Work. Admission has already reserved the bounded cancellation record, absence edges, retirement bookkeeping and cleanup Work, so these cannot become a new fallible prerequisite after a genuine loan returns. The no-fail transition marks this producer's unproduced contribution absent, transfers ownership back, destroys its unpublished payloads, releases its loan edge and original debits, and retires its occupations when other required retention/evidence obligations permit. Absence propagation over a later consumer graph remains that graph owner's charged work; the local producer's terminal record is installed now. Cancellation is possible after both invalid-payload and publication-preparation failure without replenishing the failed evaluation budget. It never cancels another loan or claims a missing requested output exists.

**Rule (Dropped capabilities and bounded reconciliation)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-abandonment`

The loan owns the sole strong `Rc` of a private immutable liveness witness; the `Out` record has only a weak witness and never upgrades it. The numerical owners and reservation tokens are destroyed before this final witness on normal drop or unwind. The next bounded heap reconciliation observes expiration, performs the pre-reserved cancellation transition with reason `Abandoned`, and releases the conservative reservation-index debit. Drop executes no heap callback and mutates no shared numerical state. Until reconciliation the producer is pending and its debit still counts; no empty-heap claim may skip this scan. Thus dropping an error's loan is an explicit abandonment path, while every consuming refusal still returns the capability for deliberate repair or cancellation. `mem::forget` can leak the owner and witness; it cannot make ownership returned, allow reuse or produce successful flush. Dropping the heap does not invalidate a loan's safe storage, but such an owner has no publication destination; dropping it releases its allocations without inventing publication or completed delivery.

## Pins, gather parents and retirement · `sec:nrz:matrix-loans-retention`

**Rule (Every live use retains the complete bundle)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-pins`

Matrix pairs each live bindings ticket with a `BundlePin` carrying strong edges to the exact value, qualified masks, required companions and layout/map owners. Bindings itself retains storage-neutral identities; the matrix application supplies their live ownership. Application inputs, host result readers, barrier inputs, mask-only continuations and delivery/evidence obligations have separately reserved use edges. `acquire_pin` and `retain_pin` report `PinGranted(pin)`, `PinWaiting(OwnershipPending)` or `PinRefused(reason)`; a copied bare ticket never adds a pin. A pin proves retained storage, not current semantic authority. A new application rechecks its declaration/source versions at admission and publication; stale assumptions refuse even though historical numerical bytes remain safely readable through an explicitly historical reader. Warm exact authority and static lowering templates own no pins under (`def:nrz:tiles-streams-region-pin`).

**Rule (Gather maps retain the parents they can read)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-gather-pins`

The existing `CellGather` records immutable original associations but contains no numerical retention authority. `bind_gather` takes that exact map and explicit qualified parent-bundle pins, authenticates each immediate parent, original source mask/layout occupation and original cell association, and constructs a `PinnedGather` with a bounded deduplicated set of strong parent edges. It reports `GatherBound(pin)` or `GatherRefused(reason)` without changing supplied parents. Composed original indices address the original storage parent, not the immediate compacted cell ordinal; the address parent is explicit. Nested maps retain both the immediate parent evidence/selection and every original numerical parent that the composed map may address. For multiple parents, a separate bounded composite map stores entries as a parent-table index plus original cell index; the table contains exact parent bundle/mask/layout identities and pins. The existing single-parent `CellGather` is the special case, not silently reinterpreted storage. Admission validates each composite entry and injectivity for compaction; ordinary repeated gathers retain their distinct read-only law. A stale metadata-only map cannot reacquire a retired parent. Maps shared by selection masks share their immutable map allocation, while all parent edges remain discoverable and counted. This closes the missing storage lifetime behind (`[MATRIX-sec:matrix:packed-cell-masks]`), (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-mask-compaction`).

**Rule (Materialization and historical associations)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-gather-release`

A gathered reader or mask-only continuation keeps its parent edges until its last reader and required evidence delivery release. During materialized compaction, the copy application pins the complete old bundles through validation and atomic publication of all copied values, masks and companions. A fully materialized output may retain original association IDs and checked immutable map/evidence metadata without retaining old floats only after it has no remaining live addressing edge to those floats and required history is retained or exported. Such a `MaterializedAssociation` cannot be used as a `PinnedGather` or upgrade an old ticket. If any downstream gather still addresses an older parent, that gather's independent edge keeps the complete older bundle alive. Source identity survives in both cases and copying never creates an independent source under (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-release`), (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-mask-compaction`).

**Procedure (Release and reclaim without erasing history)** · `alg:nrz:matrix-loans-retirement`

`release_pin` consumes the specified use edge and reports `UseReleased`; ordinary pin drop removes the same strong edge. The serialized `reclaim` pass examines registry owners in stable slot order, charging each visited slot and outgoing edge. Since public code cannot clone the underlying `Rc` or upgrade a weak reference, the serialized pass can check `Rc::strong_count` against the single registry root without races. An owner with only that root and no loan, pending producer or delivery obligation can enter `Retiring`; remove its live availability, retain or export required immutable evidence, drop its registry root and release its debit only when its payload is actually destroyed. This may release parent edges. Repeat bounded passes until no further owner can retire, or report the remaining work allowance; every pass is charged and each successful removal strictly decreases the finite owner graph. Explicit retirement of a protected owner reports `RetirementWaiting(PinnedUses)` with no premature release. Retirement outcomes are `Retired(receipt)`, `RetirementWaiting(PinnedUses)`, `RetirementWaiting(EvidenceDelivery)`, `RetirementWaiting(WorkExhausted)` or `RetirementRefused(reason)`. Historical records retain versions, exact snapshots, outcomes and availability `Retired`, but no numerical strong edges. Reserved cancellation cleanup is distinct from this general traversal. No free slot reuse erases a historical identity or revives a weak ticket.

**Rule (Observable residence and completion)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-residence`

Expose occupied numerical bytes, out-loan debits, live mask/map/initialization bytes, published and staging bytes, retention-edge/owner metadata, and reusable empty table capacity separately, keyed by occupation. Deduplicate payload owners before summing; neither dropping one pin nor moving a token is a recovered-byte event. Admission must account for outstanding loans and shared parents even during an unrelated legacy execution. Reconciliation and all required evidence delivery precede a zero-occupied report. `ResidencePending` names remaining borrower, pin, delivery or traversal work; `ResidenceEmpty` states zero occupied numerical owners after the serialized check, while reporting retained empty allocator capacity separately. This local observation supplies the storage predicate for (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-flush-completion`); it does not implement the ready-leaf walk or itself declare a full engine flush complete.

## Masked and gathered numerical application · `sec:nrz:matrix-loans-applications`

**Definition (Mask application contract)** · `def:nrz:matrix-loans-kernel-contract`

An internal `MaskedContract` retains the existing meaning-free `KernelContract` law, ordered numerical input/output extents, arithmetic revision and work admission, together with an explicitly checked lifting law from logical cells to that contract's components. It names required input/companion incidences, output channel maps, demand roles and supported gather behavior. Bindings retains the original `RegionContract` and source/reading witnesses beside it under (`rule:nrz:descriptor-binding-kernel-boundary`). Masking restricts the domain on which the same law is evaluated; it does not supply a new proof, reinterpret components as cells, lower coefficients, or waive uncertainty propagation. Pointwise lifting is admitted only for a closed recipe whose output cell depends solely on its declared input cells. A product, reduction, broadcast or stencil needs its own complete incidence law; otherwise admission returns `UnsupportedContract`, preserving (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-mask-demand`).

**Rule (Demand, availability and actual completed outputs)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-masked-law`

For requested output demand D, compute available demand A by requiring every declared input and required companion incidence to be filled under its exact target map and pinned version. For a pointwise law this is D intersected with the checked pullbacks of all required filled masks. Retain D outside A as `MissingInput`, never a successful zero or certified coverage of the omitted request. Enumerate available cells in ascending logical order and execute the same admitted scalar/component schedule on each. A cell enters the provisional completed set only after all its output components, required companions and numerical evidence succeed. Derive final completed set C and its companion masks by (`alg:nrz:matrix-loans-filled-closure`), seeded only with those successful cells; a failed cell can never reenter through component initialization alone. A provisionally completed cell removed by that closure reports `IncompleteCompanion`. `CellRefused(reason)` and `CellUndecided(reason)` remain distinct from C, and a resource-stopped suffix remains `Pending`; C and these explicit outcomes partition A when publication is attempted. The output filled mask is exactly C, not D, A or the set of locations tentatively written. Empty A executes no scalar kernel and performs no numerical loads, even though mask/version validation and output metadata still cost Work under (`rule:nrz:transcendental-region-application`).

**Rule (No read through a hole)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-selected-loads`

All numerical loads go through a selected-cell reader constructed only after mask, target and incidence validation. It supplies safe checked access to the selected initialized components and matching companions. A kernel may scan packed words and set bits; it may not present the sparse backing allocation to a backend that loads an entire rectangle, use a speculative vector load covering holes, or mask away an invalid load afterward. For a dense-only backend, pack only the selected complete incidences into fresh, initialized, reserved scratch, execute the admitted restricted law, and scatter through a checked injective output map. Packing preserves complete contraction neighborhoods and original multiplicities; it cannot delete missing terms or infer an absent-input law. Arbitrary read-only gather repetition preserves source dependence. Output destinations are disjoint and injective. Holes are zero-filled for storage safety but remain forbidden reads, including during validation, conversion and enclosure computation under (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-mask-coverage`).

**Rule (Gathered laws and complete movement)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-gathered-law`

A gathered application uses `PinnedGather` to translate demanded cells and each companion incidence before loading; it then applies the same `KernelContract` through its checked lifting/reading relation. It validates source membership, range, target versions and original associations, and preserves both covariance target maps even when their component shapes differ. A materializing gather/copy produces a new independent allocation occupation with the same source dependence; output masks derive from completely moved and validated cells. Compaction additionally validates injective new-to-original movement, full selected population and clear unused destination capacity before group publication. Reordered or repeated reads do not establish independent observations. All parent pins survive the copy and its fallible publication preparation, as required by (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-mask-compaction`).

**Procedure (Detached application admission and publication)** · `alg:nrz:matrix-loans-application`

A short heap admission authenticates inputs and qualified masks, acquires their pins, validates the numerical lifting contract and reserves unique output owners plus an application identity. `ApplicationReady(OwnedApplication)` releases the heap borrow; `ApplicationWaiting(reason)` and `ApplicationRefused(reason)` return no partially admitted application. The application owns its pins, declaration witnesses, cumulative attempt state and output initialization/evidence tracking. Evaluation needs only this owner and the caller's existing mutable Work ledger; it neither takes a legacy `Bound` nor manufactures its `NormalizedInputs`. Its closed evaluators share the established numerical arithmetic/enclosure routines through selected readers. Legacy evaluators continue to require their original execution lease. An immutable prepared input pin may be used by several separately admitted applications, each with its own output reservation and execution identity.

**Rule (Application outcomes preserve owned state)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-application-outcomes`

Evaluation reports `ApplicationCandidate(candidate)` when its admitted traversal finishes, or `ApplicationSuspended(reason, same_application)` when a resource stops it; completed and uncompleted cells remain distinguishable. Cell-level refusal/undecided outcomes are candidate evidence, not a failed ownership transfer. Retrying never resets spent Work, overwrites a published version or treats partially written output as completed. `publish_application` authenticates the execution identity and current declared dependencies and follows the recoverable preparation and no-fail commit of (`alg:nrz:matrix-loans-handback`), (`rule:nrz:matrix-loans-publication`), returning `ApplicationPublished(PublishedTile, receipt)` or `ApplicationRepair(reason, same_candidate)`. A candidate's sealed completion evidence additionally gates its initialization masks. Finalizing a suspended application's actual completed subset is explicit and still requires remaining validation/publication allowance; exhaustion promises no automatic partial publication. `cancel_application` returns `ApplicationCancelled(receipt)` or `ApplicationCancelRejected(reason, same_owner)` and releases inputs and private output through pre-reserved cleanup. No outcome discards the sole cancel capability. The family-specific semantic interpretation of numerical refusal remains with a later barrier under (`rule:nrz:transcendental-barrier-route`).

## Native Work and resource boundaries · `sec:nrz:matrix-loans-accounting`

**Definition (Execution account preserves the caller ledger)** · `def:nrz:matrix-loans-execution-account`

The execution owner moves its existing `Budget`, without cloning it, into a non-cloneable `ExecutionAccount` with a checked owner identity and private ledger. Loan and application identities retain this account identity. Public host setters and continuation operations take a short mutable account argument; the engine lends its internal `&mut Budget` to the native operations described here. They retain neither borrow between calls. The account cannot replace its ledger or reset limits/usage while continuations exist, and every retry authenticates the same account before new work. A supplied different account reports `ForeignAccount` while returning any consumed loan/application unchanged. Independent loans and legacy operations can share successive borrows of this one account while their storage owners remain out. Its identity is ownership metadata, not the tracing ledger incarnation, which is observation-only. Prepaid cancellation receipts remain attached to their original loan even when no evaluation allowance remains. This wrapper adds no second Work counter or child budget.

**Rule (One cumulative ledger and observable phases)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-ledger`

All new native steps charge the caller's existing `Budget` through `Budget::with_work_phase`, using family `matrix.loan` for admission, filling, handback and release and `matrix.kernel` for masked application. Reused mask operations retain family `matrix.mask` and the tariffs in (`[MATRIX-rule:matrix:mask-native-work]`). The same owned ledger follows repair, resumed application and cancellation; when observed, its incarnation is unchanged; no hidden child budget or reset creates free retries. Phase receipts observe the boundary without charging a second tariff. Bulk loops prepay their exact checked step count or charge immediately before each step. Failed attempts retain spent charges. Native Work is distinguished from exact Work: a selected-cell kernel invokes no CAS operation, constructs no exact descriptor and spends zero exact Work under (`rule:nrz:transcendental-region-application`). Existing full-region operations and scalar arithmetic tariffs are unchanged.

**Rule (Ownership, filling and publication tariffs)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-owner-tariffs`

Charge one Work for each request/return authentication, each owner/layout/target-version validation, each reservation or retention edge installed or removed unless that removal was prepaid under (`rule:nrz:matrix-loans-cleanup-tariffs`), each checked native incidence visited, each table slot inspected including vacancies, and each scalar component initialized, written, copied or validated. Safe zero fill pays for every addressable scalar before allocation, including unused capacity. Initialization and mask storage pay one per zeroed word; deriving per-channel completion pays one per component flag inspected and one per resulting word. Each filled-closure pass pays one per visited value cell and required incidence; mask normalization pays one per mask. Publication preparation pays one per prepared record and each scanned slot/edge, with repeated validation charged again. Pointer moves and the final no-fail commit have no additional tariff after their preparation; the coverage manifest records that boundary explicitly. Bulk setters include their stores and tracking updates in the per-component step rather than charging those twice. Clearing pays one per cleared initialization word and affected complete-cell bit. Arithmetic and count/byte overflow refuse before an unchecked count or address is used.

**Rule (Cancellation and release accounting)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-cleanup-tariffs`

At successful loan/application admission, prepay one Work per fixed cleanup record and owned allocation/retention edge that cancellation or abandonment must close, plus one for cancellation authentication and one for the terminal producer transition. Exclude pin drops already prepaid by their admission, so each edge has one cleanup entitlement. Retain this spent cleanup receipt, not a second mutable budget. Cancel authenticates using that prepaid step and performs no scan of numerical components or new allocation; a rejected foreign/ticket probe pays its own authentication on the calling operation and cannot spend the rightful cleanup receipt. Allocation destruction entitlements follow their reservation tokens into publication; later payload destruction does not debit Work again. A successful publication does not refund unused cancellation-specific authentication or absence bookkeeping. Each admitted public pin separately prepays one Work for its eventual constant-time use-edge drop, in addition to its creation charge. Explicit release and ordinary drop spend that already charged entitlement without another debit. Later registry scans and registry-root removals pay one per visited slot and removed registry edge on the caller's ledger; these are index operations and do not charge the prepaid public use-edge drop or payload destruction again. Receipts separate these prepaid destruction steps from performed traversal and each allocation/edge has one destruction entitlement. Destructors never fail because a later evaluation exhausted Work. General reclaim may remain pending under (`alg:nrz:matrix-loans-retirement`) without pretending the heap is empty.

**Rule (Mask traversal and kernel tariffs)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-kernel-tariffs`

Demand scans pay one per requested/destination word and one per participating input/companion word; mapped incidence pays one per entry inspected, including both cross-companion targets. Enumeration pays one per scanned word and one per selected cell emitted. Gathering, packing and scattering pay one per map entry checked and one per scalar component transferred; a transfer's checked load/store is included in that component charge. Direct selected reads pay one per loaded component. Outcome and completion bookkeeping pay one per evaluated cell plus one per emitted mask word; population/prefix scans and compaction keep the existing mask charges. Numerical execution additionally prepays the existing `KernelContract.work` allowance for each actual admitted invocation, once, preserving its conservative complete-phase meaning. For pointwise lifting, admission constructs a checked per-cell contract with the original component extents and recipe; it uses that contract's existing work calculation and never divides a whole-array allowance by capacity. A dense-only contraction uses its complete admitted invocation and work allowance after all incidences are packed. Empty demand invokes none. Kernels already metered by the same ledger charge through that route rather than double-debiting the allowance; admission declares which existing charge path applies. New wrapper traversal/load/store charges remain separate from that unchanged arithmetic allowance. A family without an admitted metered invocation is refused, not assigned a guessed discounted cost.

## Acceptance and implementation boundary · `sec:nrz:matrix-loans-evidence`

**Requirement (Ownership and repair controls)** · `req:nrz:matrix-loans-lifecycle-controls`

Exercise both binary32 and binary64 with independent expected occupation/debit transitions. Hold a live loan while an unrelated allocation completes an ordinary exclusive `Binding` execution and while another owned application evaluates; prove the out allocation remains unavailable and its full capacity counted. Test foreign heaps before slot indexing, stale generations, wrong nonce, duplicate completed tickets, generation exhaustion, swapped companions, changed target/layout/mask versions and invalid dependency witnesses. Public type controls establish that loans and writable views cannot be cloned, escaped or reconstructed from tickets; private invariant controls exercise otherwise unconstructible corrupt returns. Every refusal leaves the rightful producer, payload and reservation unchanged and returns the supplied owning capability where one exists. An initialized selected NaN, invalid radius or malformed declared companion must fail payload validation and return a repairable loan. A separate injected fallible publication-allocation or metadata-capacity failure after valid payload must do the same. Demonstrate repair followed by success, and each failure followed by cancellation with exhausted evaluation allowance. Check abandonment reconciliation and conservative forgotten-owner behavior, no partial visibility or dependency release, and exact once-only cleanup receipts under (`req:nrz:tiles-streams-lifecycle-evidence`).

**Requirement (Independent mask and companion oracles)** · `req:nrz:matrix-loans-mask-controls`

For capacities zero, one, 63, 64, 65 and 128, compare empty, sparse, partial and full coverage against independent logical sets and original-index/component tags. Include missing value components, complete values with missing companions, extra initialized orphan companions, repeated writes, explicit clearing, final-word padding and spare compacted cells. Use a value with multiple components and companions with unequal component counts, including a signed covariance block whose two target maps differ, to distinguish complete companion initialization from value initialization. A deliberately cross-cell incidence checks the greatest complete subset independently of the implementation's pass order. Trace selected reads and scalar invocations through an instrumented accessor: zero reads/evaluations for empty demand and no hole access for sparse demand, even when hole bytes contain invalid sentinels. Compare full and masked applications against independent evaluation of exactly the selected original cells and complete companion laws; test missing reduction operands and unsupported lifting refusal. A partial kernel failure must publish only completed cells when publication is possible and retain explicit refused/pending masks. No test may validate hole safety merely because the backing zero fill made an illegal read numerically harmless.

**Requirement (Pins, movement and bounded residence controls)** · `req:nrz:matrix-loans-retention-controls`

Retain distinct binding, reader, delivery, mask-only and nested-gather pins while releasing every other edge. Assert that each prevents reclamation of its exact value/mask/companion/map closure, including the original and immediate gather parents, and that unrelated occupations retire. Independently check original-index movement and component tags through nested and multi-parent gathers and complete compaction; test stale parent metadata, duplicate destinations, incomplete companion movement and failed group publication. Verify that fully materialized historical associations keep identity without granting old numerical access and that a separate older-region gather still prevents release. Release the last use, observe payload/debit destruction exactly once, reuse the slot with a new generation and reject all old tickets. At the engine integration boundary, failed handback followed by cancellation must permit full flush only after all readers, deliveries and evidence obligations release; an outstanding borrower must keep it unfinished. This requires (`req:nrz:tiles-streams-lifecycle-evidence`) without claiming this storage slice implements the walk or flush protocol.

**Requirement (Exact resource and accounting boundaries)** · `req:nrz:matrix-loans-cost-controls`

Carry independently computed literal Work and byte boundary fixtures at sufficient allowance and its immediate insufficient neighbor, with prior cumulative usage. Cover request, scalar/word initialization, partial writes, closure scans, handback validation, late preparation failure, repair, cancellation, mask traversal, gathered movement, pin creation and repeated reclaim passes. Inspect payload, owner/edge metadata, actual vector capacities, backend allowance, table vacancies and simultaneous staging separately. Duplicate pins must not duplicate payload charges, and a lent allocation must not disappear from unrelated operation admission. Repeat fixed-capacity admission/publication/release with bounded graph, provenance, observer and required external-history storage reported separately. Compare enabled and disabled receipt observation for identical Work and outcomes; verify zero exact per-cell Work and no descriptor/CAS calls. No acceptance result may raise a limit or overwrite an existing pin to conceal a refusal under (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-residence`), (`req:nrz:tiles-streams-accounting-coverage`).

**Decision (Smallest usable implementation slice)** · `dec:nrz:matrix-loans-first-slice`

Implement owned host input from checked fixed-capacity declarations through initialized setters, partial/empty handback, repair, cancellation, qualified filled masks, immutable reader pins and last-use retirement in the existing heap, for both native formats. Include the original-ledger execution account, per-occupation debits, abandonment reconciliation, all consuming outcomes and the foreign/stale/double, payload-failure, late-publication-failure and unrelated-execution controls with that surface. Admit identity cell maps with finite centers, deterministic total bounds and numerical-error companions; refuse other companion/incidence declarations explicitly. This is a complete usable input/read/release capability, not a kernel or scheduler claim. Follow it with the checked pointwise lifting of existing kernels, unequal-shape companion incidence and pinned gathered application described here; each extension must bring its own mask, movement and cost controls before admission broadens.

**Rule (Later engine and numerical slices)** · `rule:nrz:matrix-loans-boundary`

Lowered lambdas and their cache keys, numerical preludes and coefficient import, semantic barriers and membership decisions, the ready-leaf walk, graph-level flush, tile-size and branch-representation choice remain with (`spec:nrz:tiles-streams-design`). This design supplies their ownership, local absence, retention, application and residence interfaces without implementing their scheduling or changing their contracts. Transcendental schedules, their proof/rounding/target evidence and LC kernels remain separate consumers under (`spec:nrz:enclosed-transcendental-plans-design`). The first implementation subset does not admit arbitrary contraction/stencil incidence, statistical propagation, transport concurrency or a new endpoint conversion merely because the ownership mechanism can store their components. Those capabilities require their already specified checked laws and evidence; none is inferred from a mask, an allocation or successful handback.
