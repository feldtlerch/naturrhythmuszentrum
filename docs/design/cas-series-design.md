# Certified formal series layer design · `spec:nrz:cas-series-design-contract`

**Requirement (Design standing)** · `req:nrz:cas-series-design-standing`

This design implements the formal-series obligations of (`spec:nrz:cas-trig-series-concept`) as a specification and advertises no new executable capability. Every proposed schema remains a Requirement until implementing commits register its exact premises, conclusion, version and independent checker together with resource and rejection evidence. Existing symbolic requests, side germs, solving certificates and certified numerical enclosures keep their contracts. Formal congruence supplies no convergence radius, analytic solution, numerical error bound or source definedness at the centre.

## Objects, statements and ownership · `sec:nrz:cas-series-design-objects`

**Decision (A dedicated certified statement register)** · `dec:nrz:cas-series-design-store`

Series facts live in their own content-keyed register under a proposed `crates/cas/src/series/` module, owned by the same `Cas` lifetime and publication transaction as their graph dependencies. They do not live in the solving answer store: a product, source congruence or residual coefficient is a certified statement, not a solution answer with coverage and presentation. They do not become graph rewrites: congruence modulo a power cannot replace an original function by a polynomial. Original expressions, centres, declarations, contexts and coefficient interpretations remain references into the existing graph; the register creates no parallel expression syntax. `Statement::Germ` and its certificates remain untouched beside this register. A series-to-solving adapter constructs actual coefficient equations and supplies certificates to the existing checker without moving ownership of series facts into solving.

The dedicated series register publishes its certified statements through the anonymous-judgment mechanism with a disjoint series kind and the complete coefficient-field, centre, variable, order and context key, so the graph owns the checked payload and the register indexes only keys and node identifiers, preserving the enclosure design's rejection of a second result store (`rule:nrz:enclosures-design-reuse`), (`[CAS-def:cas:anonymous-judgment-node]`).

The register has separate immutable object keys and checked statement keys. Object interning is structural sharing, following (`def:nrz:cas-design-interning`); only successful independent checking mints a sealed statement receipt. The receipt carries store ownership and rollback generation, and references an immutable statement, its exact schema version, ordered premises, used hypotheses, residual obligations and finite certificate. A content digest may index candidates but full canonical comparison decides identity. A hash, producer cache or lookup hit is never a proof. Reuse requires the same complete expected claim and authenticated evidence; weaker-order reuse requires a checked restriction with its own claim.

**Definition (Coefficient interpretation and positive order)** · `def:nrz:cas-series-design-object`

An object records a checked coefficient field interpretation K, centre a, declared variable x, scope, complete context, positive integer n and exactly n ascending coefficients c₀ through cₙ₋₁. Its meaning is the class of P(h) = ∑ cⱼhʲ in K[[h]]/(hⁿ), where h = x − a. Centre and coefficients must be independent of x; other parameters remain explicitly scoped in the context. K includes its exact scalar space, embedding or coefficient map, checked arithmetic/equality capability and the law versions actually used. Rational coefficients are the initial executable admission; other interpretations refuse until their arithmetic, equality and required units have closed checked contracts. An exact graph constant, including Pi, is not thereby a member of K. A symbolic expression is not a unit merely because it prints as nonzero.

The complete structural key contains K and its interpretation dependencies, a, the declaration identity of x, n, the convention that n counts known coefficients, every coefficient including trailing zeros, scope and context. Checked statement keys additionally contain operation and expected endpoints, retained source domain when applicable, all used law versions, premises and obligations. Zero at order one and zero at order two have different keys and different information even when both representatives print as zero. Truncation congruence is a checked relation between objects at a specified common order, never structural equality or an unqualified expression equality.

**Rule (Explicit conversion beside side germs)** · `rule:nrz:cas-series-design-germs`

The formal order n counts known coefficients and means modulo hⁿ, exactly as (`def:nrz:cas-trig-series-object`) states. In `crates/cas/src/germ_model.rs`, `SeriesAt.order` and `GermMode::Series.order` are the largest included power m; `GermValue` contains m+1 coefficients, and `GermCertificate.remainders` supplies f(a+h) = P(h) + h^(m+1)R(h) on the checked incident sides. The side-series contract and its following remainder definition are (`tab:nrz:cas-piecewise-design-transforms`). Importing such evidence into a formal source bridge converts m to n = m+1 with checked integer arithmetic, preserves side and domain and checks coefficient interpretation. Reading a formal object as side order n−1 does not manufacture a remainder, a nonempty approach, centre ownership or analytic evidence; no reverse germ constructor is supplied.

**Definition (Requests and sealed results)** · `def:nrz:cas-series-design-api`

Integrate the series payload into the ordinary judgment API: an additive `Statement::Series` variant contains a `SeriesStatement`, and its enclosing `Judgment` carries the existing context, layer, space and residual obligations. `SeriesObject` and `SeriesCertificate` are typed payloads, not a parallel request/receipt protocol. Checked series construction and untrusted production use the ordinary application checking and sealing route and return `VerifiedWitness`; no separate five-type public API or `CheckedSeries` receipt is introduced. The expected judgment fixes the operation, all inputs and outputs, K, centre, declaration, scope, context, order convention, source domain, symbolic coefficient frame or specialization, residual equation range and used schema manifest as applicable. An independently valid different statement fails expected-claim comparison (`rule:nrz:cas-design-expected-claim`).

The existing signatures in `crates/cas/src/scoped_check.rs` remain authoritative: `Cas::replay(bytes, &Judgment, &mut Budget)` returns `VerifiedWitness`, `Cas::inspect` checks what a witness claims without selecting the caller's intended claim, and `Cas::export_witness` exports the same sealed witness type. Series inspection and replay require mutable receiving-store access and one atomic publication transaction; read-only access to a retained judgment is not a fresh acceptance operation. Ordinary source, unit, domain and coefficient proofs and series premises share this lifecycle.

## Closed schemas and certificates · `sec:nrz:cas-series-design-schemas`

**Requirement (Complete series family inventory)** · `req:nrz:cas-series-design-registration`

The proposed family is `series`, with each key below at semantic version 1; sharing that prefix with `series.case-restrict` is safe because the closed registry resolves complete keys, not prefixes. F denotes a finite exact primitive checker, T a registered theorem whose finite instance is checked, and D a derived recipe expanded into admitted primitive applications. The D key `series.divide` is not a primitive manifest entry and has no primitive certificate mode, following (`def:nrz:cas-piecewise-design-registration`). These are closed contracts, not caller callbacks. Implementing commits add exhaustive checker dispatch, formal registry entries and availability tests atomically under (`rule:nrz:cas-design-closed-registration`). A source bridge has a closed function-law mode inventory; adding a mode requires explicit registration and version review. Unavailable schemas refuse even when their syntax is representable. The existing `series.case-restrict` germ schema retains its meaning and is not a new formal-series mode. Policy weights are implementation measurements or explicit policy choices and are not invented here.

**Requirement (Closed source-lifted arithmetic clauses)** · `req:nrz:cas-series-design-source-lifting`

Extend the version-one contracts of `series.truncate`, `series.add`, `series.scale`, `series.mul`, `series.compose` and `series.reciprocal` with the source forms below; they remain F checkers of finite congruence inference, not additional primitive keys or expansion theorems. Their ordinary form concludes result tag 0 ObjectCongruence; their source-lifted form concludes tag 2 SourceCongruence for the exact original expression. Coefficient extraction remains tag 1; `series.source` supplies tag 2 from its registered theorem modes; `series.residual` supplies tag 3. No source-lifted calculus or compositional-inverse claim is implicit. Lifted composition's declared coordinate correspondence identifies the outer and inner increment rings explicitly; it changes no expansion centre without evidence. The twelve primitive certificate modes and the derived status of division remain unchanged.

A source premise is an earlier checked tag-2 judgment naming its exact source graph, coefficient interpretation, centre, declaration, scope, context, original domain, optional side and order, with a referenced series object. A lifted certificate has its existing primitive mode followed by form byte 1, the expected original composite and its ordered operand references, ordered source-premise slots, a checked tag-0 computation of that same primitive on those objects, and explicit domain/substitution proof slots. Form byte 0 is the existing object computation. Each checker reconstructs the original operator, arity and operand identities rather than trusting a printed formula or a source label. Contexts, coefficient maps, centres and orders must agree or have an explicit checked restriction; all original operands are traversed, including operands hidden by zero or cancellation. The conclusion retains the exact original composite domain and all inherited exclusions, proved domain restrictions, hypotheses and obligations; it cannot enlarge a premise domain.

Fresh replay must reconstruct the original sine(h)/cosine(h) quotient through source sine and cosine, source reciprocal and quotient assembly, then compare the expected original Div and its denominator domain. Changing Div to Mul, substituting either source operand or erasing the cosine-nonzero domain must reject even if supplied coefficient objects are unchanged.

**Rule (Precision, compatibility and inverses)** · `rule:nrz:cas-series-design-precision`

Common order is an explicit request and evidence field, not the length of a shortened printed polynomial. Addition, multiplication, composition and division use the minimum available order by default but record that decision; a caller may explicitly request a smaller positive order. Unknown coefficients never become zero. Scalar arithmetic and equality use K's admitted interpretation without silently changing field, centre, variable or context. Transport into another context or coordinate requires its own checked evidence; version one otherwise refuses. Derivative order zero is outside the object grammar, and integration reserves n+1 before construction.

Under the zero-constant and unit-linear hypotheses a one-sided compositional inverse in the truncated ring is already two-sided. Checking both directions is a deliberate independent control, not an additional mathematical assumption. The producer may solve triangular coefficient equations; the checker only checks the supplied candidate through the two independent compositions and unit evidence. Multiplicative reciprocal instead needs a unit constant and may have nonzero constant output. Its producer can use q₀=c₀⁻¹ and qₖ=−c₀⁻¹∑ from i=1 to k of cᵢqₖ₋ᵢ; checking uses the product identity, not that search recurrence.

## Source correspondence and retained domains · `sec:nrz:cas-series-design-source`

**Requirement (A source bridge is separate evidence)** · `req:nrz:cas-series-design-source-contract`

`series.source` names the original expression, its complete operand traversal, K and coefficient interpretation, a, x, scope, context, original domain D, any side restriction and exact congruence witness modulo hⁿ. A finite coefficient vector alone cannot prove correspondence to a function. The registered source law supplies the mathematical formal-expansion theorem, while replay checks its finite instance, initial values, recurrences and all source premises. Neither a differential recurrence nor agreement at finitely many samples invents that theorem. Formal correspondence does not state that the source was defined at a, does not enlarge D, and does not supply a convergence radius or analytic remainder bound.

**Table (Closed rational-origin source modes)** · `tab:nrz:cas-series-design-source-laws`

|Source in increment h|Initial coefficients and exact recurrence|Retained source obligation|
|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------|
|Exponential of h|e₀=1; (j+1)eⱼ₊₁=eⱼ.|Registered exponential formal-expansion law at zero; original operand domain.|
|Logarithm of 1+h|l₀=0; lⱼ=(−1)^(j+1)/j for 1≤j\<n, equivalently (1+h)L′=1 with that constant.|Original real domain 1+h>0 and the registered logarithm expansion at argument one.|
|Hyperbolic tangent of h|t₀=0; (j+1)tⱼ₊₁=δⱼ₀−∑ from i=0 to j of tᵢtⱼ₋ᵢ.|Registered formal law T′=1−T² with its source initial value and domain; no reuse of enclosure bounds as coefficients.|
|Sine and cosine of h|s₀=0, c₀=1; (j+1)sⱼ₊₁=cⱼ and (j+1)cⱼ₊₁=−sⱼ.|Registered paired formal origin laws and actual closed source operators; equal-spelled opaque calls refuse.|

Recurrences compute only indices below n and check every integer inverse and exact sign. All six modes initially use rational coefficients. For composite sources, checked ring operations, unit-constant division and zero-constant composition combine prior source bridges while retaining every original domain and substitution dependency. The trigonometric origin modes remain unavailable until the closed sine, cosine and arctangent operators and their source laws are registered; this design does not implement those operators.

**Rule (Coefficient admission and singularities)** · `rule:nrz:cas-series-design-source-refusals`

Sine at a+h needs only sine(a) in K at order one; at order at least two it also needs cosine(a), with further required coefficients checked at the requested order. For example, at a=Pi/6 the order-one coefficient is 1/2 in Q even though cosine(a)=sqrt(3)/2 is not rational. Version one still refuses that source bridge because no corresponding source law is registered, not because an unused cosine coefficient is mathematically required. An arbitrary exact centre supplies none of these coefficient interpretations by itself. Arctangent at 1+h has constant Pi/4, so the rational-origin mode refuses even though Pi is an exact graph constant. Additional exact fields need checked equality, arithmetic and inverses plus the corresponding source bridge. A logarithmic singularity at its zero argument and a pole cannot be represented as an ordinary positive-order power series by dropping an undefined constant or a negative power.

Cosine at zero has constant one, so a checked sine source bridge divided by a checked cosine bridge yields the formal tangent quotient while retaining the original cosine-nonzero domain. Version one refuses a cosine bridge at half Pi earlier as an unsupported source fragment because no nonzero-centre bridge is registered. A future bridge at an integer multiple of half Pi needs checked exact special values from (`req:nrz:cas-trig-series-symbolic-laws`) and a centre-shift law; at half Pi its zero cosine constant would still refuse reciprocal and quotient, with the tangent pole as the mathematical obstruction. A Laurent-series extension, with explicit negative orders and pole evidence, is a separate future proposal. For a removable expression such as sine(h)/h, the existing unit-constant division rule also refuses; a separately registered removable-continuation bridge must prove the cancellation/congruence, obtain enough numerator precision for the order loss and retain h≠0 from the original expression. It may describe a continuation without claiming that the original quotient is defined at zero.

The existing exponential, logarithm and hyperbolic-tangent evaluation schemas certify intervals with checked finite sums and tails, not formal expansions (`[CAS-thm:cas:enclosure-exp-taylor]`), (`[CAS-thm:cas:enclosure-log-atanh]`), (`[CAS-thm:cas:enclosure-tanh-compose]`). They supply no automatic `series.source` certificate. Their distinct premises, conclusions and arithmetic rounding contract cannot be reinterpreted as exact coefficient equality.

**Rule (Piecewise source evidence stays branch-local)** · `rule:nrz:cas-series-design-piecewise`

A piecewise input needs its original cover, branch domains and branch-local source witnesses, including punctured approaches and exceptional centre values. Equal finite jets never imply a common source series or a common analytic germ. A common conclusion needs a separately checked source/remainder theorem with the exact domain and centre ownership it claims; formal arithmetic alone does not merge branches. This preserves the limits rule (`rule:nrz:cas-piecewise-limits-series`) and the stronger full-polynomial-germ requirements of existing analytic side series (`tab:nrz:cas-piecewise-design-transforms`).

## Differential residuals and the consumer trial · `sec:nrz:cas-series-design-residuals`

**Requirement (Residual substitution and precision)** · `req:nrz:cas-series-design-residual-contract`

`series.residual` substitutes stated series into an original polynomial differential expression with exact coefficient interpretation and retained independent-variable expansion x=a+h. Every derivative application loses one known coefficient; with order-n inputs and maximum derivative order r<n, the uniform residual is known modulo h^(n−r), supplying exactly equations [hᵏ]R=0 for 0≤k<n−r. Products and sums are explicitly restricted to that common order. The certificate distinguishes computing a nonzero residual from proving its coefficients vanish. It retains derivative counts, initial coefficients, polynomial graph, simultaneous substitution, resulting coefficient equations and every remaining obligation. No residual congruence asserts an exact analytic solution.

**Requirement (Symbolic coefficient equations before specialization)** · `req:nrz:cas-series-design-symbolic-residual`

The free and nonlinear residual fixtures require a symbolic construction stage, distinct from an admitted Q-valued `SeriesObject`. Its input is an original polynomial differential expression and a formal coefficient template over the existing polynomial and unknown-coordinate graph, with base coefficient domain K=Q and ordered distinct unknown declarations C=(c₀,…,cₙ₋₁). For multiple dependent series, order declarations first by the request's dependent-variable order and then by ascending coefficient index; residual equations are ordered first by the original equation order and then by ascending h degree. These orders are part of the expected claim. Every coefficient unknown has a checked Real scalar sort and ambient, is independent of x and the increment, and occupies its declared unknown-coordinate slot; it is not an admitted rational coefficient value. Initial data are explicit polynomial equations or checked specialization bindings on those slots, never a silently shortened unknown frame.

This uses the existing distinction in `crates/cas/src/solving/model.rs`: `CoefficientDomainData::Rational` is not `CoefficientDomainData::Fraction { base, coordinates }`; likewise `crates/cas/src/solving/coefficients/model.rs` separates `Value::Scalar` ground scalars from `Value::Fraction` formal parameter fractions. The checked arithmetic and specialization boundary is (`def:nrz:cas-solving-design-coefficient-trait`). Version one constructs residual equations as polynomials over Q in the declared unknowns, with a checked interpretation map from base rational coefficients into the Real unknown-coordinate ambient. It does not embed unknown variables into Q, construct a fraction field in them, or assume a formal numerator is a regional unit. Parameter-dependent fractions and other coefficient domains remain unsupported by this stage unless separately admitted with their exact maps and localization evidence.

Use certificate mode 11 Residual and result tag 3 with a closed stage byte: 0 EvaluatedResidual for the existing admitted-object computation, 1 SymbolicResidual and 2 SpecializedResidual. SymbolicResidual records the base-domain reference, complete ordered unknown declarations and sorts, input coefficient-template polynomial references, centre interpretation, initial equations, equation-order metadata, coordinate/coefficient maps, and finite derivative/convolution/substitution rows giving the residual polynomials in those unknowns. The checker reconstructs every row over the polynomial graph, checks maps and scopes and compares the exact equation range; it does not require numeric series objects or treat pending equations as proved zeros. Records 39 and 40 carry the stage byte and those ordered symbolic/specialization fields after their existing result/mode discriminator; symbolic inputs use polynomial-template references instead of SeriesObject references, while the declared frame, base K and maps determine the interpretation. They refer to existing polynomial, coordinate and coefficient records and add neither a record tag nor a primitive mode.

SpecializedResidual consumes that symbolic judgment and an ordered simultaneous substitution map with a declared target. A partial map fixes lower-order coefficients, checks their exact interpreted values and equalities and retains all remaining unknown slots and their order; it yields another symbolic polynomial system, not a Q-valued series object. A complete map into K must provide a checked value for every coefficient and preserve initial data and original domain obligations before it constructs the n-coefficient series objects and checks their evaluated residual. Independently replay each mapped polynomial identity using the existing `coeff.evaluate` and `coeff.identity` contracts (`[CAS-thm:cas:primitive-coeff-evaluate]`), (`[CAS-thm:cas:primitive-coeff-identity]`); a proposed assignment is evidence of a candidate only until its residual zero claims check. Maps into symbolic free-parameter families remain graph-level family maps with their own exact source/target domains, never rational-valued series admission.

The linear adapter tests that the submitted specialized equations are affine in the complete remaining ordered unknown frame before constructing a linear system and consuming supplied row/pivot certificates. For the ordinary recursion it submits the current coefficient equation after lower-order specialization, retaining all later polynomial equations as pending; it never asserts that the full unspecialized system is affine. Nonlinear residuals such as c₁² stay real polynomial obligations; they are not coerced into exact-linear syntax. Missing bindings, wrong sorts, reordered slots, escaped parameters, unsupported coefficient interpretations, unproved localization or source-domain premises and non-affine equations retain their precise invalid, unsupported or pending/refused boundaries. Choosing numerical values for free coefficients is not a substitute for this symbolic stage or for free-family coverage. Both the free and nonlinear singular fixtures depend on this stage; their finite numerical witnesses are additional membership tests only.

**Requirement (Ordinary-centre sufficient theorem)** · `req:nrz:cas-series-design-ordinary-centre`

For r≥1, consider A(x,y,…,y^(r−1))y^(r)=B(x,y,…,y^(r−1)), with polynomial A and B over the admitted characteristic-zero field and stated coefficients c₀ through cᵣ₋₁. Require checked interpretation in K of the centred A and B after x=a+h and of all initial data, and require A₀ to be a checked unit at the centre. A checked centre a in K together with K-valued polynomial coefficients and initial data is a sufficient route to this closure; a more general route must check every actual substituted coefficient in K, including any exact cancellations, rather than infer closure from centre independence. No route silently widens K. After lower-order coefficients are fixed, residual coefficient k contains the new cₖ₊ᵣ linearly with multiplier A₀(k+r)!/k!. Derivatives below r at degree k use coefficients only through k+r−1; the new coefficient therefore enters solely through the constant A₀ multiplying y^(r). Integer ratios are exact products of k+1 through k+r, with checked units, not floating factorial approximations. This proves a sufficient triangular recursion, not linearity of the full un-specialized coefficient system or of arbitrary singular equations (`req:nrz:cas-trig-series-solving-trial`).

|y′=y with c₀=1 and order four|c₁−1=0, 2c₂−c₁=0, 3c₃−c₂=0.|Coefficients \[1,1,1/2,1/6\]; residual order three, with no equation for the unavailable c₄.|
|y″=y with c₀=1, c₁=0 and order five|2c₂−1=0, 6c₃−c₁=0, 12c₄−c₂=0.|Coefficients \[1,0,1/2,0,1/24\]; residual order three verifies the factorial multiplier and two derivative losses.|
|h y′=y at a=0, order four|Unknown frame (c₀,c₁,c₂,c₃); equations −c₀=0, 0=0, c₂=0 for j=0,1,2.|Rank and augmented rank two; pivot columns 0 and 2, complete free-columns list \[1,3\]. Both c₁ and c₃ are free. This singular linear system is outside the ordinary-centre theorem. The finite solution 7h+9h³ is accepted because its residual 18h³ vanishes modulo h³.|
|y′=x at a=2, c₀=0 and order three over Q|c₁−2=0 and 2c₂−1=0 after the checked substitution x=2+h.|Admit \[0,2,1/2\]; the centre, initial data and centred coefficients all have checked rational interpretations.|
|y′=x at a=Pi, c₀=0 over Q|The constant residual equation would require c₁=Pi although A₀=1 is a unit.|Refuse unavailable rational coefficient interpretation before invoking the recursion; no silent widening of K.|
|y y′=1 with c₀=1 and order four|c₁−1=0, 2c₂+c₁²=0, 3c₃+3c₁c₂=0.|Admit \[1,1,−1/2,1/2\]; nonconstant A=y has unit A₀=1. The full symbolic system is nonlinear, while each successive lower-coefficient specialization leaves a linear new-coefficient equation.|
|h y′=y at order four, paired initial data c₀=0 and c₀=1|After fixing c₀, keep ordered unknowns (c₁,c₂,c₃): the first branch has 0=0, 0=0, c₂=0; the second has −1=0, 0=0, c₂=0.|At c₀=0, rank and augmented rank one, free columns \[0,2\] corresponding to original \[1,3\]. At c₀=1, rank one and augmented rank two; retain the contradiction, refuse a surviving solution and reject removal of the contradiction row.|
|y y′=0 with c₀=0 and n≥3|Constant coefficient is c₀c₁=0; degree-one coefficient is c₁²+2c₀c₂=c₁².|Retain the real nonlinear pending obligation c₁²=0. The exact-linear adapter refuses this branch unless a separately checked consequence has already specialized it. Never replace it by an unsupported linear certificate.|

At n≥3 the h y′=y fixture constrains c₀ and c₂ through cₙ₋₂ but leaves both c₁ and the terminal coefficient cₙ₋₁ free. No statement that higher available coefficients vanish sets the known terminal input coefficient to zero: that would assert an equation outside the certified range. `crates/cas/src/solving/linear_check/system.rs` reconstructs every nonpivot column and compares the complete `free_columns` list; fixing or omitting c₃ in the order-four fixture rejects fabricated full coverage even though a chosen particular solution can satisfy the equations.

Multiplication by h can mathematically recover one order in the order-four singular fixture; version one deliberately promises only the uniform n−r range. A future precision-strengthening schema would need its own proof of the additional coefficient. Partitioning a leading coefficient into zero and nonzero cases preserves both branches but does not make the zero branch exact-linear. The nonlinear fixture must remain visible in the acceptance evidence rather than be discarded by choosing a generic branch.

**Decision (Supplied certificates establish the trial)** · `dec:nrz:cas-series-design-linear-trial`

The consumer trial supplies reversible row operations and pivot witnesses to the existing exact linear-equivalence checker. The conceptual primitive table (`tab:nrz:cas-solving-primitives`) and checked theorem (`[CAS-thm:cas:primitive-linear-equivalent]`) are the interface, not an assumption of automatic linear-family production. `crates/cas/src/solving/linear_check/mod.rs` reconstructs matrices, executes supplied row operations, compares the full result and checks pivot/rank evidence and the original statement. The adapter must retain coefficient-domain maps, original regions, definedness, zero-pivot partitions, augmented contradictions and free parameters. A bounded trial never replaces a symbolic pivot by a numerical tolerance.

Acceptance retains the actual residual system before and after lower-order substitution, its coefficient interpretation and precise equation range, the supplied certificates and unit witnesses, independently checked results, free-parameter scopes and every precise refusal. The ordinary fixtures exercise the supported linear route; the singular fixtures separately exercise a free coefficient and a nonlinear obligation. An optional external residual fixture may extend that evidence, but has no assigned supplier and cannot replace these mandatory controls or claim a new solver capability.

## Portable evidence and resources · `sec:nrz:cas-series-design-portability`

**Decision (Explicit additive wire extension)** · `dec:nrz:cas-series-design-wire`

This design allocates against the sole current witness format specified by the CAS guide (`[CAS-sec:cas:guarantees]`). `wire_decode.rs` and `wire_objects.rs` admit records through tag 37, with 37 used for a certified context in `wire_records.rs`. Allocate next-free record tags 38 through 41 below as an explicit additive extension of that envelope; do not renumber existing records or alter their payloads. Each new record uses schema 1 and the existing bounded length, Logic layer and absent distribution-space prefix. Existing readers reject the new tags; new readers accept old supported records unchanged. Unknown record schema, family version, mode or manifest entry refuses before publication; an unsupported law version is never redirected to a newer checker. If another implementing change occupies a proposed tag first, reconcile the allocation explicitly before registration rather than alias meanings.

**Table (New record inventory and ordered payloads)** · `tab:nrz:cas-series-design-wire-records`

|--------------|---------------------------------------|
|38 SeriesObject|Coefficient interpretation reference; centre reference; variable declaration reference; scope; context reference; convention byte 0 meaning coefficient count; positive order u32; coefficient count u32 equal to order; ordered coefficient references.|

All references point backward to appropriate record kinds in a bounded dependency DAG. Schema-specific required fields and vector extents follow the registry table: ring rows contain indices and exact operands/results; inverse rows include both compositions; source rows carry recurrence initial data and domain evidence; residual rows retain the differential expression and exact coefficient range. Coefficients use the existing canonical scalar or coefficient-value representations under K. Canonical ordering compares complete descriptors after dependency indices are assigned, preserves ordered coefficients and premises, and sorts only declared sets. Decode checks counts, multiplication overflow and remaining payload before allocation; replay reconstructs every mathematical claim, compares the complete caller expectation and checks byte-for-byte canonical re-encoding before atomic import.

The closed statement result tags are 0 ObjectCongruence, 1 CoefficientValue, 2 SourceCongruence and 3 ResidualCongruence. Primitive certificate modes are 0 Truncate, 1 Add, 2 Scale, 3 Mul, 4 Coefficient, 5 Compose, 6 Invert, 7 Reciprocal, 8 Derive, 9 Integrate, 10 Source and 11 Residual; each primitive schema admits only its own mode. Division requests reconstruct their expected quotient from the expanded reciprocal and multiplication claims; no `series.divide` statement, certificate mode or primitive manifest entry is emitted. Source-law modes are 0 ExponentialOrigin, 1 LogOnePlusIncrement, 2 TanhOrigin, 3 SineOrigin, 4 CosineOrigin, 5 ArctangentOrigin and 6 ExistingSideGerm. The last consumes the exact existing germ certificate with the order conversion above; polynomial and composite source bridges expand checked operation and source dependencies rather than inventing another function-law mode. No removable-continuation mode is registered by this design. Scalar unit evidence names both the element and inverse and checks their product is one in the identical interpretation and context.

`Operator::Series`, `Task::Series`, `Statement::Germ`, germ statement/certificate discriminants and `SeriesAt` encodings do not acquire this meaning. The ordinary judgment API and dedicated series register supply new records without reusing a solving answer tag or a generic equality relation. Ordinary graph proof dependencies continue through the existing wire machinery; new series records participate in its bounded canonical closure through an explicit typed dependency adapter. Fresh replay has no producer cache and grants no authority to serialized success flags, following (`[CAS-sec:cas:guarantees]`).

**Requirement (One portable judgment lifecycle)** · `req:nrz:cas-series-design-wire-lifecycle`

Record 39 decodes to the series payload of an ordinary typed `Judgment`; record 41 is the additive series-conclusion encoding and decodes to the existing `Decoded::Conclusion(VerifiedWitness)` category after checking its series statement and certificate. Thus the root check in `crates/cas/src/wire_decode.rs` still requires a Conclusion, but admits a checked series conclusion against `Statement::Series` as well as existing ordinary conclusions. Record 41 is not a separate public receipt or a syntax-only root. Existing ordinary statement and conclusion record payloads remain unchanged; a mixed ordinary application that uses a series premise names its earlier checked conclusion through an explicitly kind-checked premise reference.

Ordered premise slots in records 40 and 41 accept ordinary checked application/conclusion references and checked series-conclusion references, according to the schema's typed premise shape. Decoding reconstructs receiving-store graph objects, contexts, scopes and proof ownership; sender handles, cache identities and serialized success flags have no authority. The complete dependency DAG, including ordinary unit/domain/identity proofs and series computations, checks before sealing. `Cas::replay` compares the reconstructed `Judgment` against the caller's independently built receiving-store expectation before the transaction publishes any imported record; an error rolls back the entire new mixed closure while retaining spent Work. `Cas::inspect` can report the checked claim without substituting for that expectation.

`Cas::export_witness` and `VerifiedWitness::exported_byte_len` retain their existing signatures and meaning for series witnesses. The complete canonical mixed closure is exported, with exact staged, witness and output byte reservations and one Work per copied byte; callers retaining an earlier output use `Budget::with_retained_payload` so returned buffers remain accounted separately from graph retention and scratch. Foreign-store witnesses refuse. Independent positive controls include a hand-authored canonical golden witness containing all four new record kinds 38–41 and an ordinary-proof dependency, decoded and checked against an independently constructed expected judgment, and unchanged successful replay of supported old ordinary records. The golden witness must not be generated by the production exporter; self-export followed by replay is a separate lifecycle control, not its replacement.

**Rule (Cumulative ledger and atomic publication)** · `rule:nrz:cas-series-design-budget`

Use caller-owned cumulative Work with prior spend intact through production, checking, replay, reuse and export. A new phase does not reset the allowance or borrow a default budget. Keep Work, storage occupancy, peak scratch, component/intermediate bits, object order, traversal depth and output ceilings distinct. Reuse state is an explicit precondition of each measurement: fresh store, retained exact hit and checked restriction are different operations. Failed admitted work remains charged; rejected reservations do not invent completed work. Atomic rollback removes all newly staged objects, keys, evidence and speculative storage without removing earlier committed facts or refunding spent Work. No partial checked receipt escapes on exhaustion.

Implementing commits measure each complete named operation at a successful ceiling N and show refusal at N−1 with identical inputs, prior spend, reuse state and all other ceilings. Measure full inverse production plus two-way checking separately from supplied-candidate checking; measure complete import including expected-claim comparison and publication separately from export. Record the exact exhausted counter and rollback assertions. A first refused charge is not the successful cost, and no figure is invented here (`def:nrz:engineering-budget-meaning`), (`def:nrz:engineering-budget-boundary`).

## Independent verification and landing · `sec:nrz:cas-series-design-verification`

**Decision (Four existing test layers)** · `dec:nrz:cas-series-design-test-layout`

Use inline module-private tests only for inaccessible internals, crate interaction tests in `crates/cas/src/tests/`, public conformance modules in `crates/cas/tests/conformance/`, and full client lifecycles in `crates/cas/tests/integration/`, registered through the existing `tests/public.rs` target. Shared setup belongs in `src/testing/`. Put each assertion in the outermost layer able to express it, as (`dec:nrz:cas-solving-design-test-layout`) requires. Independent arithmetic oracles use small bounded exact integers/rationals and are not calls to the producer, checker or shared series recurrence under test.

Hand fixtures include (1+2h+3h²)(4+5h)=4+13h+22h² modulo h³, composition of h+h² with h−h² yielding h−2h³ modulo h⁴, and inverse of h+h² equal to h−h²+2h³−5h⁴ modulo h⁵. At order six, tanh and arctangent have respective h⁵ coefficients 2/15 and 1/5; require an independent tanh oracle obtained by hand-expanding (exp(2h)−1)/(exp(2h)+1) with exact polynomial products and quotient coefficient equations, separately from the tanh recurrence and replay checker. Passing producer-to-checker replay alone is insufficient evidence for shared arithmetic correctness.
